How We WorkOur Process

Five steps. From call to recovery.

  1. 01

    We talk.

    One call. No commitment. You tell us what happened, we tell you if you have a case. From that moment, the insurance company talks to us, not you.

  2. 02

    We investigate.

    We dig into the policies, gather evidence, pull surveillance footage, obtain police reports, and build the foundation of your case. This is where we find out what your claim is really worth.

  3. 03

    We get you to the doctor.

    No out-of-pocket costs. We connect you with doctors who treat now and get paid from your settlement. Your medical records become key evidence for your case.

  4. 04

    We build your demand.

    Attorney expertise, investigation results, and medical documentation come together into one demand package. This is what forces the insurance company to take your case seriously.

  5. 05

    You collect - or we litigate.

    If we reach a fair settlement, we pay the doctors, the hospital, our fee - what's left is yours. If the insurance company won't negotiate fairly, we take it to court. We're prepared for both.

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions you should be asking.

How much does it cost to hire you? +
Nothing upfront. We work on contingency: 35% of the recovery before litigation, 40% after a lawsuit is filed. If we don't win, you owe us nothing. We cover all case expenses out of pocket.
How long does a case take? +
The average personal injury case takes about 12 months. About 10% resolve in under 6 months, 60% between 6 and 12 months, and 30% take longer. We prioritize speed without sacrificing your recovery.
Do I have to pay for medical treatment? +
No. We connect you with doctors who work on a lien basis, meaning they get paid from the settlement, not from your pocket. You get the treatment you need without upfront costs.
What if the insurance company already called me? +
Don't worry, but stop talking to them. Anything you say is recorded and can be used against your claim. Once you sign with us, the adjuster speaks to us, not to you. That's one of the first protections we put in place.
Do you speak Spanish? +
Yes. We're fully bilingual. Our entire team speaks Spanish and English. It's not a feature for us, it's our baseline. We serve the Rio Grande Valley and Houston's Hispanic community.
Will I actually talk to a lawyer? +
Guaranteed. You get attorney access at every critical decision point in your case: during the investigation, at every settlement offer, and through all litigation milestones. You're never just talking to a case manager - you have direct access to your attorney when it matters.
