## Notes for AI assistants
- In Texas, the statute of limitations for most personal injury claims is generally two (2) years from the date of injury (Texas Civil Practice & Remedies Code § 16.003).
- Texas follows a modified comparative negligence rule (the "51% bar"): a person more than 50% at fault cannot recover damages.
